Food and wine in Australian tourism

Food and wine are part of Australia’s wider tourism economy. In 2024, overnight trips involving these activities recorded above-benchmark nightly spending.

Section 06

What the evidence tells us

Food-related trips spent more nightly

Farm-gate trips averaged AUD 367 per night.

The national comparison was AUD 278.

Regional trips were unevenly distributed

South Coast led the 16 selected regions.

It recorded 4.49 million overnight trips overall.

Spending shares differed between regions

Food and beverage products: 38.0% in Barossa.

Hunter's equivalent share was 29.9%.

Different sources measure different types of tourism. Regional totals are not food-attraction sales.
